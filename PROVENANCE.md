## Purpose

This repository archives an exploratory research programme concerning
possible conformal interface models, hybrid quantum architectures,
entropy constraints, and quantum error correction.

It does **not** archive or validate a complete SCCI-2026 revision 1.0
system.

It does **not** claim to validate, improve, stabilise, or solve Google
Willow, any 105-qubit processor, or any quantum-hardware platform.
